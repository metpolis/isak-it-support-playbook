<h1>Jira</h1>

<p>Jira is one of my most important tools in my daily work as IT support. It is the system where I receive and manage all my tickets.</p>

<hr>

<h2>📌 Overview</h2>

<p>Jira allows me to organize my work and maintain a clear overview of both my own tasks and what my colleagues are working on. This makes it easier to prioritize and collaborate within the team.</p>

<p>It also provides a structured way of documenting issues and solutions as I work, while giving me access to previous tickets. This makes it possible to learn from earlier cases and reuse solutions for similar problems.</p>

<hr>

<h2>🧰 Daily usage</h2>

<p>In my daily workflow, I mainly use different queues to manage tickets.</p>

<p>- All open tickets</p>
<p>- My open tickets</p>

<p>These views allow me to focus on my own tasks while still keeping an eye on the overall workload. A large part of my day is spent working within these queues and handling incoming issues.</p>

<hr>

<h2>🛠️ How I handle a ticket</h2>

<p>When working on a ticket, I follow a structured approach to ensure consistency and quality in my support:</p>

<p>- Read and understand the issue clearly before taking action</p>
<p>- Check if similar tickets have been solved before</p>
<p>- Reproduce the issue if possible</p>
<p>- Start with basic troubleshooting (restart, connectivity, permissions)</p>
<p>- Investigate further using logs, error messages, or system tools</p>
<p>- Apply a solution step-by-step</p>
<p>- Test to confirm the issue is resolved</p>
<p>- Document the solution in the ticket</p>
<p>- Close the ticket or follow up with the user if needed</p>

<p>This approach helps me stay structured and ensures that both the solution and the process are documented for future reference.</p>

<hr>

<h2>⚙️ Strengths</h2>

<p>- Provides a clear overview of tasks and team activity</p>
<p>- Enables continuous documentation of work and solutions</p>
<p>- Makes it easy to reference previous tickets and learn from them</p>
<p>- Strong alignment with ITIL practices, making it suitable for structured IT support environments</p>

<p>Because of this, Jira is a future-proof ITSM tool that can scale with both small and large IT teams.</p>

<hr>

<h2>⚠️ Limitations</h2>

<p>While Jira is a powerful tool, it does have some drawbacks in daily use.</p>

<p>One of the most noticeable issues is performance. The system can become slow, especially when working with large queues or many tickets.</p>

<p>Additionally, integrations with other systems could be improved. While this is often a “nice to have,” some other ITSM platforms offer more seamless integrations out of the box.</p>

<hr>

<h2>📈 Personal experience</h2>

<p>Jira is my first ITSM system, and I find it relatively easy to get started with. However, mastering it takes time, especially when it comes to workflows, automation, and advanced usage.</p>

<p>Overall, it is a central tool in my daily work and an important part of how I manage and document IT support tasks.</p>
