<h1>Confluence</h1>

<p>Confluence is used as our internal documentation platform within IT, as well as a public-facing space for colleagues outside the department.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Overview</h2>

<p>Confluence serves as a centralized place for documentation, making it easier to share knowledge across the organization.</p>

<p>We maintain both internal IT documentation and a public space where other departments can access information such as FAQs, guides, and contact points for support.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Page structure</h2>

<p>A Confluence page is built using different sections and layout elements.</p>

<p>- Sections can be divided into columns</p>
<p>- Content can be structured using panels, tables, and dashboards</p>
<p>- Pages can be designed to be both simple and highly interactive</p>

<p>This flexibility allows for structured and user-friendly documentation.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Macros</h2>

<p>Macros are a key part of how Confluence pages are built and customized.</p>

<p>They can be used for many purposes, including:</p>

<p>- Embedding dynamic content</p>
<p>- Creating visual elements such as panels and tables</p>
<p>- Integrating with other systems</p>

<p>In my case, I mainly use macros that integrate with Jira, such as ticket overviews, graphs, and submission forms.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Permissions and access</h2>

<p>One of the most important aspects of Confluence is managing permissions correctly.</p>

<p>Access is typically controlled through Active Directory (AD) groups.</p>

<p>- IT department: Full access to internal documentation</p>
<p>- Other departments (e.g., HR): Access to public spaces only</p>

<p>The public space is used for ticket submissions, FAQs, tips and tricks, and guidance on who to contact for specific issues.</p>

<p>Ensuring correct permissions is critical for both security and usability.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Use case</h2>

<p>Confluence is a strong tool for:</p>

<p>- Internal IT knowledge sharing</p>
<p>- Creating structured documentation</p>
<p>- Providing a user-friendly support portal for non-IT staff</p>
