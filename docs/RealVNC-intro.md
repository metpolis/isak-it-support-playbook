<h1>RealVNC Viewer</h1>

<p>RealVNC Viewer is an important tool in daily support operations, especially for remote support in retail environments.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Overview</h2>

<p>RealVNC Viewer allows me to remote into devices such as POS systems and other store machines without requiring active involvement from store employees.</p>

<p>This is especially useful because different employees may not always know the technical procedure required for troubleshooting or remote support.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Remote support</h2>

<p>In most cases, employees only need to confirm:</p>

<p>- The device is powered on</p>
<p>- The device is connected to the network</p>

<p>Once this is confirmed, I can handle the remaining troubleshooting and maintenance remotely through RealVNC Viewer.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Use cases</h2>

<p>Common use cases include:</p>

<p>- Maintenance and troubleshooting of POS systems</p>
<p>- Remote support for store devices</p>
<p>- Verifying systems running Smartsign for marketing and TV advertisements</p>

<p>This allows issues to be resolved quickly without requiring physical presence in the store.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Summary</h2>

<p>RealVNC Viewer is a key tool for remote support, allowing efficient troubleshooting and maintenance of critical retail systems from off-premises locations.</p>
